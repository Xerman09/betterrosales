Gateway of the Ilocos Region

The Crossroads of Heritage and Progress

Strategically positioned at the end of TPLEX Phase 1 and the MacArthur Highway, Rosales is a 1st-class municipality spanning 37 barangays. Home to nearly 67,000 residents, it serves as the vibrant eastern gateway to the Ilocos Region. Whether you are craving the legendary tupig of Carmen, exploring centuries-old architecture, or investing in our rapidly growing commercial hubs, Rosales offers a perfect balance of rural charm and urban convenience.

Brief History of Rosales

1852

Rosales was formally founded as an independent municipality on November 28, 1852 by Royal Decree, named in honor of Don Antonio Rosales.

1853

Originally established within the jurisdiction of Nueva Ecija, the town was formally annexed to the province of Pangasinan due to thriving trade routes and river navigation.

1902

Organized under the American civil administration, rapidly developing as the primary agricultural and marketplace center connecting the central plains to northern Luzon.

1939

The completion of the historic Carmen Bridge spanning the Agno River established Rosales as the irreplaceable transport crossroads and gateway of Eastern Pangasinan.

Present

Flourishing as a premier 1st-Class Municipality and regional economic power, hosting major regional transport terminals, SM City Rosales, and 37 progressive barangays.

Gateway of Eastern Pangasinan

Strategically connecting the provinces of Pangasinan, Tarlac, and Nueva Ecija, Rosales serves as the vital junction for regional travel, commerce, and agriculture.

Modern Commercial Hub

Home to major commercial complexes, banking hubs, modern public markets, and premier health facilities serving over 67,000 residents and neighboring towns.
